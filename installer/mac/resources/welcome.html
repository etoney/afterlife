Afterlife @VER@

The Echoes Never Die. A convolution reverb and delay with section lanes, by Castle Danger Music Company.

This installs Afterlife as a VST3 and an Audio Unit, universal for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs (macOS 11 or later), into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins, and its quick guide into /Applications/Castle Danger Music Company/Guides.

No Terminal commands are needed: the installer clears the download flag itself and resets the Audio Unit cache.

If you installed Afterlife by hand in your own plug-in folders, that copy is moved aside to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins Disabled/Castle Danger (older copies) so your DAW doesn't see two.
