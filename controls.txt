Afterlife is a convolution reverb and a delay on one insert, with two lanes
underneath: REVERB and DELAY. The knobs set how each effect sounds through
the whole song. The lanes let you change one part of it, like a word or a
phrase, by marking a SECTION.

Tip: double-click a knob to reset it. Double-click the value under a knob to
type an exact number.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
REVERB
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SPACE         The room the reverb is built from:
                  CHAMBER      a small stone room, tight and natural
                  CRYPT        low stone ceiling, boxy and cold
                  CATACOMB     long tunnels, the walls flutter back
                  CATHEDRAL    huge, a slow swell and a long dark tail
                  CISTERN      an underground tank, concrete and water, rings
                  IRON PLATE   a steel plate, instantly dense and bright
                  MAUSOLEUM    a marble hall, bright and polished
                  THE VOID     no walls at all, blooms in and lingers
                  LOADED IR    the impulse response you loaded
  LOAD IR       Load your own impulse response (WAV, AIFF or FLAC, up to 16
                seconds). The file is remembered with the song. If you move
                it, the screen says IR MISSING; load it again.
  ON            Reverb on / off.
  SEND          How much of the track goes into the reverb, between
                sections. The REVERB lane's sections override it.
  DECAY         How long the tail takes to die away (60 dB). With LOADED IR
                it trims the IR's tail. Set it long to hear the whole file.
  PRE-DELAY     A gap before the reverb starts, so the words stay clear in
                front of it.
  WIDTH         Stereo width of the reverb: 0% mono, 100% normal, 150% extra
                wide.
  LOW CUT       Takes the mud out of the reverb.
  HIGH CUT      Darkens the reverb.
  DUCK          Pushes the reverb down while the track is playing, so it
                blooms in the gaps.
  LEVEL         The reverb's level in the mix.

  The screen shows the space's decay. Its lamp lights while a REVERB
  section is playing, next to how much is going in right now.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DELAY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  SYNC / FREE   SYNC: the delay time follows the song's tempo (TIME is a note
                value: 1/64 to 1/1, with dotted "." and triplet "T").
                FREE: TIME is in milliseconds.
  STEREO / PING / MONO
                STEREO: left echoes left, right echoes right.
                PING: the echoes bounce from side to side.
                MONO: one echo in the middle.
  ON            Delay on / off.
  TIME          The delay time.
  FEEDBACK      How many times the echo repeats.
  TONE          Darkens each repeat a little more.
  LOW CUT       Thins each repeat a little more.
  DIRT          Tape grit: saturation, wow and flutter on the repeats.
  DUCK          Pushes the echoes down while the track is playing, so they
                answer in the gaps.
  SEND          How much of the track goes into the delay, between sections.
  LEVEL         The delay's level in the mix.

  The screen shows where the echoes land and how they fade. Cream marks the
  main echo (the knobs) and yellow marks the last throw (a DELAY section).
  Its lamp lights while a DELAY section is playing.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE LANES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Both lanes show your track's waveform with the song's bars (or seconds)
  along the top. The dashed line is the knob's SEND: what the lane does
  between sections.

  MAKE A SECTION     Drag across a lane. Double-click a lane for a section
                     one beat long.
  MOVE IT            Drag the middle of it.
  RESIZE IT          Drag its left or right edge.
  HOW MUCH GOES IN   Drag its top line up or down (or use SEND under SECTION).
  DELETE IT          Select it and press DELETE / BACKSPACE, use DELETE
                     under SECTION, or right-click it.
  RIGHT-CLICK        Full send, half, KILL (nothing goes in), the throw's
                     time and feedback, copy to the other lane, delete. On
                     an empty spot: add a section, or clear the lane.

  REVERB lane   A section sets how much goes into the reverb while it plays:
                more reverb on one word, or KILL for none.
  DELAY lane    A section sends the track into a THROW echo instead of the
                main one, with its own TIME (or the TIME knob) and FEEDBACK.
                The throw keeps ringing with those settings after the
                section ends, while the main echo carries on with the knobs.

  Sections only control what goes INTO the effects, so a reverb tail or a
  throw never cuts off when the section ends: it rings out.
  FADE (under SECTION) fades each section in and out so nothing clicks.

  FOLLOW        The lanes scroll with your DAW's playhead. Scrolling, or
                dragging the ruler, turns it off. Click FOLLOW to turn it
                back on.
  SNAP          Section edges snap to the beat, 1/8 or 1/16 of the song's
                grid, or not at all.
  - / +         Zoom out / in. Ctrl / Cmd + scroll zooms around the mouse.
  SECTIONS      Sections on / off. Off: both effects follow their knobs all
                the way through (handy for comparing).
  FORGET WAVE   Clears the waveform drawn so far. Your sections stay.

  THE WAVEFORM
  As an insert, a plugin can only hear what the DAW plays through it, so
  Afterlife draws the waveform as the song plays. Play the song through
  once. The waveform is saved with the song.
  With ARA (Fender Studio Pro: drag Afterlife onto the clip itself; Logic,
  Cubase, Reaper: as an ARA extension), Afterlife reads the clip as soon as
  it's applied and draws the whole thing at once. The status screen says
  "ARA // 1 OF 1 CLIP READ".

  Sections are placed on the song's timeline (in seconds). If you move the
  vocal clip later, move its sections too.
  Sections only play while the song is playing. When the transport is
  stopped, the knobs rule.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OUTPUT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  DRY           The untouched track. Set it to 0% when Afterlife is on an FX
                bus (or pick the "FX Bus: 100% Wet" preset).
  OUTPUT        Final output trim.
  WET gauge     (top left) How much reverb + delay is coming out.


-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRESETS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Presets only set the knobs. Your sections belong to the song and a preset
  never touches them. User presets are saved here:
      macOS:    ~/Library/Application Support/Castle Danger/Afterlife/Presets
      Windows:  %APPDATA%\Castle Danger\Afterlife\Presets
